Instant AI explanations

Point the camera at any homework page. Our AI reads it, understands the subject, and delivers a clear, age-appropriate explanation in seconds — no typing required.

  • Works with maths, science, languages & more
  • Step-by-step guidance for tricky problems

Section-by-section audio

Each part of the explanation has its own play button. Kids can listen to exactly the bit they're stuck on, as many times as they need, without restarting the whole thing.

16 languages

From English and Spanish to Arabic and Japanese — Snappy Study explains homework in the language your child is most comfortable with, supporting bilingual and multilingual families.

Parent activity feed

See every scan your children have done, with timestamps and a preview of what was explained. Stay close to your child's learning without hovering over their shoulder.

Every image is checked before it's analysed. Snappy Study only works on genuine educational content — photos of pets, people, or random objects are politely refused.
